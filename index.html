Strategies

Your playbook for Win, Place and Show bets: how you pick a horse, which numbers you trust, when to sit a race out. Keep each note short so you can check it at the track. Exotic strategies live in Exotic mode.

Your playbook for exotic wagers: how you build Exacta, Trifecta, Superfecta, Super High Five and Pick tickets, such as who to key, when to box, and which races to spread in. Use Edit categories to group notes by wager or approach. Win / Place / Show notes live in Basic mode.

Risk Matrix

The Risk Matrix decides how much of your bankroll to stake on each bet. Each tier starts at a bankroll size and sets a risk %, and your suggested stake on the Tracker is your balance × that %. A small bankroll bets a big share so it can grow; as it grows the % drops, so one bad day can't wipe out what you've built. Use Edit tiers to make it more or less aggressive.

Exotic wagers at a glance

Approximations, not real data. The min bets and average payouts below are rough estimates, not figures from any track or TwinSpires. Min bets vary by track, and payouts swing widely from race to race. Use Edit numbers to replace them with what you actually see.

Vertical

Every pick is in one race: you're calling the order horses finish in. Exacta, Trifecta, Superfecta and Super High Five.

Horizontal

The ticket carries across several races in a row, and you need the winner of each one. Pick 3 and Pick 5.

Click a column to sort. Hover over (or tap) a bet's name to see how it works. Possible outcomes is plain math: every result a ticket could need with this many horses in each race. Break-even is how often a min-bet ticket has to hit to pay for itself at the average payout (1 in ROI).
